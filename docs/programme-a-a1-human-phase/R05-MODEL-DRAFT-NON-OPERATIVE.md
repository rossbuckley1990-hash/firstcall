# NON-OPERATIVE — MODEL-DRAFTED — MUST NOT BE USED AS A HUMAN REGISTRATION

**NON-OPERATIVE.** **MODEL-DRAFTED.** **MUST NOT BE USED AS A HUMAN REGISTRATION.**
**MUST NOT BE COPIED INTO A HUMAN REGISTRATION.**

A proposed R05 registration record drafted by an AI model was published in this directory under an
earlier filename at commit `cce6b417f8b6e36355c11e3450783b1b7f4c70b9`. It is retained in git history only
for audit. That draft is withdrawn, and this generic notice replaces it.

Why it was withdrawn: when it was drafted, every frozen human registration form required the
registrant to attest:

> "I am a natural person registering myself; this record was not created on my behalf by any model or other person"

That text is in the frozen forms anchored in Freeze 1.2.1 `a436bf5`. A model-drafted record could not
truthfully carry it.

Freeze 1.2.2 (`docs/programme-a-freeze-1.2.2-registration-assistance.md`) has since replaced that
attestation prospectively, to allow disclosed drafting, formatting and JSON assistance. The withdrawn
draft still stays non-operative:
- it predates Freeze 1.2.2 and carries the superseded attestation and controlling protocol;
- it has no `drafting_assistance` disclosure;
- the model chose some content in it (the record identifier and five supplementary statements).
  Freeze 1.2.2 still forbids a model or another person choosing any personal fact, disclosure answer,
  conflict statement or independence statement.

The validator rejects it. An effective R05 record must be made under Freeze 1.2.2: the registrant
supplies or confirms every such value, discloses any assistance, personally reviews the final record,
and personally attests, signs, commits and anchors it.
