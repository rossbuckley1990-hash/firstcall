# NON-OPERATIVE — MODEL-DRAFTED — MUST NOT BE USED AS A HUMAN REGISTRATION

**NON-OPERATIVE.** **MODEL-DRAFTED.** **MUST NOT BE USED AS A HUMAN REGISTRATION.**
**MUST NOT BE COPIED INTO A HUMAN REGISTRATION.**

This file previously held a proposed R05 registration record drafted by an AI model (commit
`cce6b417f8b6e36355c11e3450783b1b7f4c70b9`, retained there only for audit). That draft is withdrawn.

Why: every frozen human registration form requires the registrant to attest:

> "I am a natural person registering myself; this record was not created on my behalf by any model or other person"

That text is in `experiments/programme-a/a1/registrations/forms/R05-R06-adjudicator.form.json`,
anchored in Freeze 1.2.1 `a436bf5`. The form's `signature_method` is "annotated tag or signed commit
made by the registrant personally". The frozen pre-reveal audit
(`experiments/programme-a/a1/registrations/pre-reveal-audit.json`) states that "each human record must be
made and signed by the person". The frozen materials supply a blank template (`TEMPLATE_UNFILLED`).
They do not permit a record filled or drafted by a model or another person. A record derived from the
withdrawn draft could not truthfully carry the attestation.

The effective R05 record must be made personally by the registrant from the frozen blank form. The
registrant types every variable value and writes any optional statements, then commits and anchors it.
